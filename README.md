Privacy Policy for Old Horizons

Information Collection



One Studio does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.



The game does not require user registration or account creation.

The game may save progress, settings, and other gameplay data locally on your device. This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

Old Horizons does not request access to sensitive device features such as your contacts, camera, microphone, or location unless explicitly required by a future feature and approved by the user.



Third-Party Services



At this time, Old Horizons does not use third-party services that collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or user tracking.



If third-party services are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.



Children's Privacy



Old Horizons is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
